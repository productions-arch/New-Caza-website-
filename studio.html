001 — WHY WE EXIST

The screen was the great theatre of the last hundred years. We intend to make it monumental again.

The moving image once held the whole culture in a single room. It set the terms of how we dreamed, argued, and remembered. Then the feed flattened it — endless, disposable, forgettable by design.

CAZA exists to reverse that. We back stories told with craft, patience, and nerve: work that commands attention rather than competing for it, and earns a permanent place in the canon rather than a moment in the scroll.
