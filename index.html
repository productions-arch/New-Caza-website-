001 — THE STUDIO

We are restoring the moving image to its original dominance.

CAZA is a film and television studio built for the next era of storytelling. We believe the screen — the great theatre of the last hundred years — has been flattened by the feed. We exist to make it monumental again.

Every project begins with a single conviction: that a story told with craft, patience, and nerve can still command a room, a culture, a generation. We develop, produce, and finance original films and series with the discipline of a gallery and the ambition of a movement.
